The centrepiece. Two distinct stories that may want splitting later:

  1. Cutting — layout on the timber, boring and chopping mortises, sawing
     tenons, the jigs, how long a joint actually takes, the one cut in the
     wrong face.
  2. Raising — the single best day of content in the project. Plan the
     photography in advance, because on the day you will not be thinking
     about the camera. Someone else on a second camera is worth arranging.
